Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Frank Assistant ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner with Google accounts the owner controls.

Data the app accesses

With the account holder's consent, the app uses Google APIs to read Gmail messages and settings and Google Calendar events, and, for the assistant's own account only, to send email, manage filters and create calendar events.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide assistant features to the owner: summarizing and triaging email, answering questions about the calendar, and sending messages the owner asks for.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Email and calendar content is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used to train general-purpose AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
